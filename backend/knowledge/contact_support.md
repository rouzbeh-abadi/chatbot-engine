# Contacting SkyDesk Support

## When to Contact Support

Customers should contact SkyDesk support when an issue cannot be
completed through **My Trips** or when the booking requires manual
review.

Typical reasons include:

-   payment completed but no booking confirmation;
-   refund status problems;
-   airline cancellation with no self-service option;
-   urgent booking changes;
-   name correction requests;
-   ticketing errors;
-   missing booking information;
-   technical problems managing a trip.

## Self-Service First

For faster resolution, customers should first check **My Trips** for
available actions such as:

-   viewing the itinerary;
-   checking booking status;
-   cancelling an eligible booking;
-   requesting a flight change;
-   adding baggage;
-   selecting seats;
-   viewing refund status;
-   finding check-in information.

## Information to Provide

Customers should provide:

-   SkyDesk booking reference;
-   passenger name;
-   email address used for the booking;
-   a short description of the issue;
-   relevant flight date or flight number.

Payment-related cases may require the transaction amount and last four
digits of the payment card.

## Sensitive Information

Customers must never send:

-   full payment card numbers;
-   CVV/CVC security codes;
-   online banking passwords;
-   account passwords;
-   one-time authentication codes.

Support will never request these values.

Passport or identity documents should only be uploaded through an
approved secure document-upload flow when specifically required for a
booking change.

## Support Tickets

When an issue requires manual investigation, the assistant can create a
support ticket after collecting the required non-sensitive information.

A ticket should contain a clear category and summary so that it can be
routed correctly.

Example categories include:

-   Booking Change
-   Cancellation
-   Refund
-   Payment
-   Ticketing
-   Baggage
-   Check-in
-   Seat
-   Technical Issue

## Urgent Travel

A case is considered time-sensitive when departure is within 24 hours
and the customer cannot travel as booked because of a ticketing,
schedule, check-in, or booking-management issue.

Urgent classification does not guarantee that an airline will approve a
requested change.

## Airline vs SkyDesk Support

Some issues are controlled directly by the airline, particularly airport
operations, boarding decisions, baggage handling, and day-of-travel
disruptions.

SkyDesk support can help identify the appropriate airline and provide
booking information, but cannot override airport or airline operational
decisions.

## Expected Communication

Customers should keep their booking email address accessible because
confirmations and updates may be sent there.

When a case is under airline review, SkyDesk should communicate the
known status rather than promise a resolution time that has not been
confirmed.

## Creating a Ticket

A support ticket should be created when:

1.  self-service cannot resolve the issue;
2.  the request requires manual investigation or airline contact;
3.  the required booking information is available;
4.  the customer has not provided prohibited sensitive data.

The customer should receive a ticket reference that can be used for
future follow-up.
