# Flight Cancellation Policy

## Overview

Whether a booking can be cancelled and whether money can be refunded
depends on the fare rules attached to the ticket. SkyDesk does not apply
one universal cancellation rule to all flights.

Customers can review the cancellation conditions displayed during
checkout and in **My Trips → Fare conditions**.

## Customer-Initiated Cancellation

Customers who no longer wish to travel can request cancellation from
**My Trips → Manage booking → Cancel booking** when self-service
cancellation is available.

A cancellation request is not the same as a confirmed refund. After the
request is submitted, SkyDesk checks the ticket conditions and displays
or communicates the applicable refundable amount.

Some fares are fully refundable, some allow cancellation for a fee, and
some are non-refundable.

## Cancellation Fees

A refundable ticket may still have:

-   an airline cancellation penalty;
-   a SkyDesk service fee where disclosed during the cancellation flow;
-   non-refundable optional services;
-   currency conversion differences.

The cancellation summary should be reviewed before the customer confirms
the request.

## Non-Refundable Tickets

A non-refundable fare normally does not return the base ticket price
after voluntary cancellation. Certain unused government or airport taxes
may still be refundable depending on the ticket and applicable rules.

Customers should not assume that cancelling a non-refundable booking
creates travel credit. Credit is available only where the fare
conditions or airline explicitly permit it.

## Airline-Initiated Cancellation

If the airline cancels a flight, the customer may have different rights
from a voluntary cancellation. Depending on the itinerary and airline
options, the customer may be offered:

-   rebooking on another flight;
-   an alternative itinerary;
-   a refund for the affected unused flight;
-   other options required by applicable passenger-rights rules.

SkyDesk will display available options when they are received from the
airline.

## Significant Schedule Changes

A major schedule change may qualify for airline-approved alternatives
even if the original fare was non-refundable. Eligibility depends on the
airline's schedule-change policy and the extent of the change.

Minor timetable changes do not automatically create a right to cancel
without penalty.

## Partially Used Bookings

If part of an itinerary has already been flown, cancellation and refund
calculations can be different. The value of the used segment may be
recalculated under the fare rules.

Customers should contact support rather than cancelling the remaining
itinerary without review.

## Multiple Passengers

When several passengers are on the same booking, self-service
cancellation may apply to all passengers. If only one passenger wants to
cancel, the booking may first need to be split by the airline.

Customers should carefully review the names shown in the cancellation
confirmation screen.

## Separate Tickets

If a journey contains separate ticket numbers, cancellation of one
ticket does not automatically cancel the others. Each ticket is governed
independently.

## Before Cancelling

Customers should verify:

1.  which passengers and flights will be cancelled;
2.  the estimated refundable amount;
3.  whether optional services are refundable;
4.  whether they would prefer a date change instead;
5.  whether travel insurance may cover the reason for cancellation.

Once a ticket has been cancelled, reinstatement is generally not
guaranteed.
