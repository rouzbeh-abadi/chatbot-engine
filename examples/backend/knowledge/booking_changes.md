# Booking Changes

## Overview

Changes to a confirmed flight booking depend on the airline's fare rules
and seat availability. A ticket that allows changes may still require
both a change fee and payment of any fare difference.

Customers should use **My Trips → Manage booking → Change flight** when
self-service changes are available.

## Date and Time Changes

When changing a flight date or departure time, the new itinerary is
repriced using current availability.

The total change price can include:

-   airline change fee;
-   difference between the original and new fare;
-   taxes associated with the new itinerary;
-   disclosed service fees.

If the new fare is cheaper, the difference is not automatically
refundable unless the fare conditions explicitly allow it.

## Flexible Tickets

A flexible fare generally reduces or removes the airline's change
penalty. It does not guarantee that changes are free because a fare
difference may still apply.

## Origin and Destination Changes

Most airline tickets do not permit arbitrary changes to the origin or
destination. Where routing changes are allowed, restrictions can apply.

A customer wishing to travel to a completely different destination may
need to cancel the original ticket and make a new booking.

## Passenger Name Changes

Customers must enter passenger names as they appear on their travel
documents.

Minor spelling corrections may be possible. Complete transfer of a
ticket to another person is generally prohibited by airlines.

Name correction requests should be submitted as soon as an error is
noticed. SkyDesk may require a copy of the relevant travel document
through a secure upload process.

## Airline Schedule Changes

If the airline changes the departure time, flight number, route, or
operating schedule, alternative options may become available without the
normal voluntary change fee.

Eligibility depends on the severity of the schedule change and the
airline's policy.

## Changes Close to Departure

Airlines can restrict changes after check-in or shortly before
departure. Customers should request changes as early as possible.

Missing a flight without changing or cancelling it beforehand can result
in a **no-show** status. On some tickets, a no-show can also cancel
later segments in the itinerary.

## Round Trips and Connecting Flights

Flight segments in one ticket can be linked. Changing one segment may
require repricing the entire ticket.

Customers should not intentionally skip an outbound or connecting
flight. Airlines may automatically cancel subsequent segments.

## Separate Tickets

Changes to one separately ticketed flight do not automatically protect
or modify another booking. If a customer changes one flight, they are
responsible for ensuring that connections on separate tickets remain
possible.

## After a Change

Once a change is confirmed, SkyDesk issues an updated itinerary.
Customers should check:

-   passenger names;
-   dates and local departure times;
-   airports and terminals;
-   baggage allowance;
-   connection times;
-   new ticket numbers where applicable.
