# Check-in Guide

## Overview

Check-in is normally performed with the operating airline. SkyDesk
provides booking information and, when supported, links customers to the
airline's check-in service.

The operating airline can differ from the airline shown as the marketing
carrier on a codeshare flight.

## Online Check-in

Online check-in commonly opens between 24 and 48 hours before departure,
but the exact period depends on the airline and route.

Customers should use **My Trips → Check-in** to view available
instructions. If SkyDesk cannot provide a direct check-in link, the
customer should use the operating airline's website or app.

## Information Required

Customers may need:

-   airline booking reference;
-   passenger surname;
-   passport or identity document details;
-   destination address for certain countries;
-   visa or travel authorization information.

The SkyDesk booking reference is not always the same as the airline
booking reference. Both may appear in the booking details.

## Airport Check-in

Customers who cannot check in online should arrive early enough to
complete check-in at the airport.

Recommended arrival times are at least:

-   2 hours before most short-haul flights;
-   3 hours before most long-haul international flights.

Airline and airport requirements take priority over these general
recommendations.

## Check-in Deadlines

Airlines close check-in before scheduled departure. Arriving at the
airport before departure does not guarantee acceptance if the check-in
deadline has passed.

Customers are responsible for meeting check-in, baggage-drop, security,
immigration, and boarding deadlines.

## Boarding Passes

A mobile boarding pass may be available after online check-in. Some
airports or routes require a printed boarding pass or document
verification at a desk.

Customers should follow the instructions provided by the airline.

## Unable to Check In Online

Online check-in can be unavailable because of:

-   required passport or visa verification;
-   special assistance;
-   travelling with an infant;
-   airline technical restrictions;
-   schedule changes;
-   airport-specific requirements;
-   incomplete passenger information.

Failure of online check-in does not necessarily mean the booking is
invalid.

## Codeshare Flights

For codeshare bookings, check-in usually takes place with the airline
actually operating the aircraft. The booking details identify the
operating carrier.

## Changes After Check-in

Changing or cancelling a ticket after check-in can require the airline
to remove the check-in status first. Customers should contact support or
the airline if self-service management is blocked after check-in.

## Travel Documents

Successful online check-in does not confirm that a passenger meets
immigration requirements. Passengers remain responsible for carrying
valid travel documents.
