# Seat Selection

## Overview

Seat selection availability and pricing are controlled by the operating
airline. SkyDesk can offer seat selection on supported bookings, but a
requested seat remains subject to airline confirmation.

## Selecting a Seat

When available, customers can select seats during booking or later
through **My Trips → Manage booking → Seats**.

Seat maps show the seats currently made available by the airline. Not
every unoccupied seat on the aircraft is necessarily available for
advance selection.

## Paid Seats

Airlines may charge for:

-   standard advance seat selection;
-   front-row seats;
-   extra-legroom seats;
-   exit-row seats;
-   preferred zones.

Seat prices can change and are normally charged per passenger and per
flight segment.

## Free Seat Assignment

If a customer does not purchase a seat, the airline generally assigns
one during check-in. SkyDesk cannot guarantee that passengers on the
same booking will be seated together.

Families should check the operating airline's family seating rules.

## Exit Rows

Exit-row passengers must satisfy the airline's safety requirements.
Restrictions commonly apply to children, passengers travelling with
infants, passengers requiring certain forms of assistance, and anyone
unable to perform emergency duties.

The airline may reassign an exit-row seat if eligibility requirements
are not met.

## Aircraft Changes

Airlines can change aircraft for operational reasons. A seat number may
therefore change even after it has been confirmed.

When possible, the airline will assign an equivalent seat, but an
identical location cannot be guaranteed.

## Seat Refunds

Seat fees are generally non-refundable after voluntary cancellation or
change unless the airline's conditions state otherwise.

If the airline cannot provide a purchased seat because of an operational
change, the customer may be eligible for a refund of the seat fee.

## Special Seating Needs

Customers requiring accessibility-related seating should request special
assistance rather than relying only on paid seat selection.

## Multiple Flight Segments

Seats are selected separately for each flight segment. Purchasing a seat
on one flight does not reserve the same seat number or type on
connecting flights.

## Problems with Seat Selection

If payment succeeds but the seat remains unconfirmed, customers should
avoid purchasing the same seat again immediately. Check **My Trips**
first and contact support if the status remains pending.
