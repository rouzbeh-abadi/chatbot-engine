# Payments and Payment Issues

## Overview

SkyDesk accepts supported payment methods displayed during checkout.
Availability can depend on the customer's country, booking currency,
itinerary, and transaction value.

A booking is confirmed only when SkyDesk displays a confirmation and
issues a booking reference. A bank authorization by itself does not
guarantee that a ticket was issued.

## Card Payments

Customers should ensure that:

-   the card is enabled for online and international transactions;
-   billing information is correct;
-   sufficient funds or credit are available;
-   authentication requests from the bank are completed.

SkyDesk uses payment providers to process card details and does not
require customers to send complete card information to support.

## Payment Declined

A declined payment can be caused by the issuing bank, authentication
failure, spending limits, fraud controls, incorrect card information, or
temporary payment-provider issues.

SkyDesk support usually cannot see the bank's exact reason for a
decline.

Customers can try again after confirming the transaction with their bank
or use another payment method.

## Payment Pending

A pending transaction is not necessarily a completed charge. Banks may
temporarily reserve funds while a payment attempt is being processed.

If SkyDesk did not issue a booking confirmation, customers should check
**My Trips** before making another purchase.

Temporary authorizations are normally released by the bank
automatically. Release timing is controlled by the bank.

## Charged but No Booking Confirmation

If the customer's bank shows a completed charge but SkyDesk has not
issued a booking reference, the customer should contact support.

Support will request safe identifying information such as:

-   customer name;
-   email used during checkout;
-   approximate payment time;
-   amount and currency;
-   last four digits of the card.

Customers must never provide a full card number, card security code,
online banking password, or one-time authentication code.

## Duplicate Charges

Two visible transactions can represent one completed payment and one
temporary authorization. Customers should first check whether both
transactions have fully settled.

If two completed payments exist for the same booking, support can
investigate and arrange correction where appropriate.

## Price Changed During Payment

Flight prices and availability are dynamic. A fare can disappear between
search and ticket issuance.

If the airline price changes before confirmation, SkyDesk will not
intentionally charge the customer the higher price without displaying
the updated amount for approval.

## 3-D Secure Authentication

Some cards require additional authentication through the issuing bank.
The customer must complete this process directly with the bank.

SkyDesk support will never ask for a one-time password or authentication
code.

## Payment Security

Customers should only enter payment information in the official SkyDesk
checkout flow.

Support agents must not request:

-   full card numbers;
-   CVV/CVC codes;
-   bank passwords;
-   one-time passwords;
-   authentication app codes.

Suspicious requests should be reported through the official support
channel.
