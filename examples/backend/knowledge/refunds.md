# Refund Policy

## Overview

Refund eligibility is determined by the ticket's fare rules, the reason
for the refund, and whether the ticket has been used. SkyDesk processes
eligible refund requests with the airline or ticketing provider.

A refund request may pass through several states: **requested**, **under
review**, **submitted to airline**, **approved**, **processing**,
**completed**, or **rejected**.

## Refundable Fares

For refundable fares, the amount returned is calculated after any
applicable airline penalty and disclosed service charges.

Optional products such as seat selection, baggage, insurance, priority
services, or lounge access can have separate refund conditions.

## Non-Refundable Fares

Non-refundable fares generally do not permit a refund of the base fare
after voluntary cancellation. Some unused taxes may remain refundable.

SkyDesk support cannot override airline fare rules simply because a
customer can no longer travel.

## Airline Cancellations

When an airline cancels an eligible flight and a refund is selected
instead of rebooking, the normal voluntary cancellation penalty does not
apply to the affected unused flight.

For itineraries with several segments, the refundable scope depends on
how the flights are ticketed and whether remaining travel still serves
its original purpose.

## Refund Processing Time

After SkyDesk marks a refund as completed, the money is returned to the
original payment method whenever possible.

Typical processing expectations are:

-   card payments: 5--10 business days after the refund is released;
-   some debit cards and international cards: up to 15 business days;
-   bank-based payment methods: timing depends on the banking provider.

Airline approval can take additional time before SkyDesk is able to
release the refund.

## Original Payment Method

For fraud prevention and accounting reasons, refunds are normally sent
to the original payment method.

If the original card has expired or been replaced, the issuing bank can
often route the refund to the replacement account. Customers should
contact their bank if a completed refund is not visible.

## Partial Refunds

Partial refunds can occur when:

-   only some passengers cancel;
-   only part of the itinerary is unused;
-   the fare is non-refundable but taxes are refundable;
-   an optional service is refundable separately;
-   an airline approves only the affected flight.

The refund confirmation provides the final amount once calculation is
complete.

## Currency Differences

A refund is processed in the booking's transaction currency where
possible. If the customer's bank account uses another currency, the bank
may apply its own exchange rate.

As a result, the amount visible on the customer's statement can differ
from the amount originally charged after currency conversion.

## Duplicate or Incorrect Charges

Customers who believe they were charged twice should first check whether
one transaction is a temporary authorization.

If two completed charges exist for the same SkyDesk booking, support
should be contacted with the booking reference and the last four digits
of the payment card. Full card numbers must never be sent through chat
or email.

## Chargebacks

Customers should contact SkyDesk before initiating a payment dispute
when possible. A chargeback can prevent or delay an existing refund
process because the payment becomes subject to the card provider's
dispute procedure.

## Tracking a Refund

Refund status can be viewed under **My Trips → Payments & refunds** when
available.

Support can explain the recorded status but should not promise a bank
arrival date once funds have left SkyDesk.
